## Professional profile

Ph.D. geneticist with professional experience in molecular and cytogenetic diagnostics, laboratory operations, research, and scientific education. Doctoral research investigated the functional role of a HER2-associated regulatory enhancer in breast cancer using CRISPR/Cas9, integrating experimental molecular genetics with bioinformatic interpretation.

My current portfolio also includes independent, project-based work in computational genomics and reproducible analysis workflows, including WES, viral genomics, RNA-seq, and molecular-dynamics simulation. These computational projects are presented separately from my professional diagnostic-laboratory experience.
