## Selected research and computational projects

**HER2 enhancer functional genomics**  
CRISPR/Cas9-based investigation of a HER2-associated enhancer and lncRNA GAS5 in SKBR3, MCF7, and HEK293 cell lines, integrating molecular experiments with bioinformatic interpretation. Findings published in peer-reviewed journals.

**Whole-exome sequencing workflow — project-based**  
Nextflow DSL2 workflow using fastp, BWA-MEM, SAMtools, GATK, and VEP with a GRCh38 reference. [GitHub](https://github.com/RojhanGenomics/human-wes-pipeline)

**Viral genomics workflow — project-based**  
Reproducible Nextflow workflow for public Illumina Ebola virus data (SRR1553428), with QC, alignment, variant calling, and consensus generation; repository reports 100% genome coverage and 561 SNPs. [GitHub](https://github.com/RojhanGenomics/viral-genomics-nextflow)

**TP53–PRIMA-1 molecular-dynamics project — work in progress**  
Docking-to-MD workflow using AutoDock Vina and GROMACS. A citable project snapshot is archived at [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22330272).

**Additional projects:** [RNA-seq differential-expression analysis](https://github.com/RojhanGenomics/RNA-seq-Differential-Expression-Analysis) · [WES variant-interpretation case study](https://github.com/RojhanGenomics/WES-variant-interpretation-case-study) · [PRS literature review](https://github.com/RojhanGenomics/PRS-literature-review)
