GROMACS + AutoDock Vina pipeline investigating how PRIMA-1 (a small-molecule reactivator of mutant p53) interacts with the p53 core domain (PDB 2OCJ). Blind and focused docking, ligand force-field parameterization (GAFF/acpype), system solvation, energy minimization, and NVT/NPT equilibration are complete; production MD and full trajectory analysis (RMSD, RMSF, hydrogen bonding, MM-PBSA, clustering) are currently in progress.

**Tools:** AutoDock Vina, RDKit, GROMACS 2020.1, PyMOL

**Status:** 🚧 Work in Progress
