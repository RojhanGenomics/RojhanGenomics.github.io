A Streamlit-based multilingual (Persian, English, Arabic — with RTL support) digital mental health screening application. Uses a PHQ-4 screener, a pseudonymous ID system with a daily-submission lock, and a Google Sheets backend via a scoped service account. Includes a full ethics and methodology document and comprehensive documentation.

**Status:** Submitted to the Digital Public Goods Alliance (ID: GID0093928, Under Review)

**Tools:** Python, Streamlit, Google Sheets API
